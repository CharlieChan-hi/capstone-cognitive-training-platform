## Project Story

Capstone Cognitive Training Platform is a cognitive training and assessment platform for training games, baseline assessment, history, analytics, leaderboard, profile, and admin views.

The public story should emphasize:

- research-style cognitive training
- measurable sessions and trial-level data
- local MySQL/TiDB-compatible persistence
- privacy-aware handoff and documentation
- stable AI-assisted maintenance
