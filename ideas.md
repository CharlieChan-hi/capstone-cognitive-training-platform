<idea>
  **Design Movement**: **Swiss Style (International Typographic Style) with a Clinical Modern Twist**
  
  **Core Principles**:
  1. **Objectivity & Precision**: The interface should feel like a precision instrument. Every element has a clear purpose, no decoration for decoration's sake.
  2. **Grid-Based Order**: Strict adherence to a mathematical grid to convey stability and scientific rigor.
  3. **Content-First**: The data (charts, metrics, stimuli) is the hero; the UI recedes to the background.
  4. **Accessibility & Clarity**: High contrast, clear legibility, and distraction-free environments for cognitive tasks.

  **Color Philosophy**:
  - **Clinical White & Slate**: A base of sterile white (#FFFFFF) and cool slate grays (#F8FAFC to #0F172A) to evoke a laboratory setting.
  - **Functional Accents**: Use specific colors only for semantic meaning (Green for Go/Correct, Red for No-Go/Error, Blue for Action).
  - **Intent**: To minimize cognitive load outside the tasks themselves, ensuring the user's attention is 100% on the training.

  **Layout Paradigm**:
  - **Asymmetric Split**: Use a fixed left sidebar for navigation and user status, with a wide, expansive content area on the right.
  - **Modular Cards**: Dashboard and analytics presented in clearly defined, sharp-edged cards with subtle borders, not shadows.
  - **Whitespace as Separator**: Use generous padding instead of lines to separate sections.

  **Signature Elements**:
  - **Monospace Data**: All numerical data and metrics displayed in a high-quality monospace font (e.g., JetBrains Mono or Roboto Mono) to emphasize precision.
  - **Thin, Sharp Lines**: 1px borders in slate-200 for structure.
  - **Status Indicators**: Small, pulsing dots or clear badges to show system status (e.g., "Recording", "Syncing").

  **Interaction Philosophy**:
  - **Instant Feedback**: Interactions (clicks, hovers) are snappy and immediate (0ms-100ms duration).
  - **Deliberate Actions**: Critical actions (like starting a test or deleting data) require distinct, conscious interaction.

  **Animation**:
  - **Minimal & Functional**: No decorative motion. Transitions are simple fades (opacity 0 -> 1) or slides.
  - **Stimulus Precision**: Stimuli in games appear/disappear instantly without easing to ensure timing accuracy.

  **Typography System**:
  - **Headings**: **Inter** (Bold/SemiBold) - Clean, neutral, legible.
  - **Body**: **Inter** (Regular) - Optimized for screen reading.
  - **Data/Metrics**: **JetBrains Mono** - For all timestamps, scores, and reaction times.
</idea>

  **Design Movement**: **Neumorphism (Soft UI) Refined for Medical Tech**
  
  **Core Principles**:
  1. **Tactile Realism**: Elements should feel like physical buttons and panels on a medical device.
  2. **Soft Focus**: Gentle shadows and highlights to reduce eye strain during long sessions.
  3. **Calm Concentration**: The environment should induce a flow state, not anxiety.
  4. **Human-Centric Data**: Presenting complex data in a way that feels approachable and organic.

  **Color Philosophy**:
  - **Off-White Base**: A very light grey-blue base (#F0F4F8) to reduce glare compared to pure white.
  - **Soft Shadows**: Using multiple drop shadows (light and dark) to create depth.
  - **Calming Blues & Teals**: Primary colors are soft blues and teals, associated with focus and mental clarity.
  - **Intent**: To create a "safe space" for training where failure is part of the process, reducing performance anxiety.

  **Layout Paradigm**:
  - **Central Focus**: A "stage" layout where the active task or main chart takes center stage, floating above the background.
  - **Floating Controls**: Navigation and tools float in a dock-like structure or detached panels.

  **Signature Elements**:
  - **Soft Rounded Corners**: Large border-radius (16px-24px) for a friendly, organic feel.
  - **Inset Inputs**: Input fields that look "pressed in" to the surface.
  - **Glassmorphism Overlays**: Subtle frosted glass effects for modals and floating headers.

  **Interaction Philosophy**:
  - **Physicality**: Buttons appear to depress when clicked.
  - **Smooth Flow**: Transitions are fluid and eased (cubic-bezier) to mimic physical movement.

  **Animation**:
  - **Gentle Easing**: All movements have slow-in, slow-out easing.
  - **Breathing Effects**: Subtle pulsing for "ready" states.

  **Typography System**:
  - **Headings**: **Nunito** or **Quicksand** (Rounded sans-serif) - Approachable and modern.
  - **Body**: **Lato** - Humanist sans-serif for readability.
  - **Data**: **Fira Code** - Monospace but with a softer character.
</idea>

  - **Intent**: To make the user feel like they are engaging with a high-tech system, boosting motivation through gamification aesthetics.

  **Layout Paradigm**:
  - **Dashboard Density**: High information density, utilizing the full screen width.
  - **Grid Systems**: Visible grid lines and rulers.

  **Signature Elements**:
  - **Glowing Edges**: Subtle glows on active elements.
  - **Tech Borders**: Corner brackets and technical markings.
  - **Gradient Text**: For key metrics and headers.

  **Interaction Philosophy**:
  - **Gamified Feedback**: distinct visual rewards for high performance.
  - **Keyboard First**: Optimized for keyboard navigation and shortcuts.

  **Animation**:
